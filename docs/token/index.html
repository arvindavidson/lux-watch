Send token to watch

Puts your Muse SDK token on Lux Watch over Bluetooth, so you don't have to type it on the watch.

  1. On the watch, open Lux Watch → Settings → SDK token. Leave it on that screen.

  2. Copy your token from gadgets.muse.ai and paste it here.

  3. Tap Send, then pick your watch.

The token goes straight from this phone to the watch, encrypted. This page doesn't store it or send it anywhere else. Needs Chrome on Android. Lux Watch is unofficial and not made by Meta.
